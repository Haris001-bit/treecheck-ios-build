Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TREECHECK for contractors

How It Works
What is TREECHECK?
TREECHECK is an AI-powered lead generation platform for tree care professionals. Homeowners submit a photo of their tree, our AI analyzes the job, estimates costs, and delivers the lead directly to qualified contractors in the area — complete with customer contact info, job details, and the original photo.
How do I receive leads?
Leads are delivered instantly through the TREECHECK app (a lightweight web app you install on your phone). You'll get a notification whenever a new lead drops in your service area. Open it, review the details, and unlock the customer's contact info to follow up.
What information comes with each lead?
Every lead includes:
  • Customer name, phone number, and email
  • Photo of the tree submitted by the homeowner
  • AI-identified job type (removal, trimming, stump grinding, etc.)
  • Estimated cost range based on the AI analysis
  • City / location
How are service areas determined?
When you register, we automatically set up your service area to cover the OKC metro — roughly 50 cities and towns within 30 miles of downtown Oklahoma City. You can add or remove individual cities anytime from your dashboard.
Pricing & Credits
How much does it cost to unlock a lead?
Lead pricing is based on a small percentage of the AI's estimated median job cost — not a flat fee. On the Free tier, the unlock fee is 5% of the median estimate. For example, a job estimated at $800–$1,200 (median $1,000) would cost $50 to unlock.

Premium members pay 5% when they're in the early-access rotation (getting the 30-minute head start), but only 2.5% on leads outside of their rotation window — so that same $1,000 lead would cost just $25 when out of rotation.
Is there a monthly subscription?
No monthly subscription is required. TREECHECK uses a prepaid credit system. You load credits onto your account and they're deducted as you unlock leads. There are two ways to add credits:
  • Standard Credit Refill — $250: Your first lead unlocks at the discounted 2.5% rate; subsequent leads are charged at the standard 5% rate.
  • Premium — $500: All leads unlock at 2.5%, plus you get a 30-minute head start on new leads before other contractors see them.
What happens when my credits run out?
On the Free/Standard tier, you simply purchase another credit refill when you're ready. On Premium, your account automatically recharges $500 to your saved payment method so you never miss a lead or lose your early-access perks.
How many contractors will I be competing with?
You'll never be competing against more than two other tree companies. Each lead is capped at 3 contractors — once three have unlocked it, it's closed for good.

On top of that, every contractor in the TREECHECK network is verified and insured, and our AI generates cost estimates based on arborist-approved pricing ranges. That means homeowners already have realistic expectations before you show up — dramatically reducing the chance of losing a job to a low-baller who undercuts on price. You're competing on quality, not racing to the bottom.

Premium members with early access get a 30-minute head start, giving them the best shot at being one of the three.
Can multiple contractors unlock the same lead?
Yes, but it's limited to 3 contractors per lead. After three pros have unlocked it, the lead closes. Premium members in the early-access rotation see leads first, so they have the best chance of claiming a spot.
Premium Membership
What does Premium include?
Premium ($500 credit purchase) gives you three major advantages:
  • 30-minute early access: You see new leads before non-Premium contractors.
  • 2.5% unlock rate: Half the standard fee on every lead.
  • $500 in credits: Loaded to your account immediately.
How does the 30-minute head start work?
When a new lead comes in, the top 3 Premium members in the rotation are notified first. They have a 30-minute window to review and unlock the lead before it opens up to all contractors in the area. The rotation cycles fairly so every Premium member gets their turn.
Can I start on Free and upgrade later?
Absolutely. You can register on the Free tier and upgrade to Premium at any time from your dashboard. Your credits and history carry over.
Getting Started
How do I sign up?
TREECHECK is invite-only. Once approved, you'll receive a personalized registration link via email. Click it, set your password, choose your plan, and you're in. The whole process takes about 2 minutes.
Do I need to download an app?
TREECHECK is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — no app store download required. After registering, you'll be prompted to add it to your home screen. It looks and feels like a native app with push notifications and offline support.
What if I need help or have more questions?
Reach out anytime — you can contact us directly through the app or email our team. We're a small operation and respond quickly.
